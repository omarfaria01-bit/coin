Rewards & draw

100% of fees go back to the community: 67% holder rewards, 28% prize pot, 5% buyback & burn. 0% to the team.

The team holds about 2% of the supply.

Proof: the dev cannot take the fees

  • 100% of the coin's creator fees go to the public system wallet 3ann…hJkd. Its key was generated on our server by the fee bot and is held only there.
  • Fee setting on pump.fun: transaction link added right after launch.
  • A bot spends the fees by fixed public rules (rules file, 72h timelock). The 5% for buyback & burn is held in the system wallet until the burn starts.
  • Every move is public: system wallet on Solscan and the bot's log fee-bot.json.
  • Team wallets earn no rewards and receive 0% of fees.
  • Honest limit: the bots run on our server; full on-chain enforcement (multisig with time lock) is planned.

The dev does not run the money

Fees and payouts are handled by two automatic bots: fees are claimed and split every hour, rewards are paid every day. No transaction needs the dev's signature, and the dev never chooses who gets paid or how much. The rules are public code and every transaction is on-chain, so anyone can check that what happens matches the rules. The only manual control is an emergency stop that pauses everything for everyone; any use is announced publicly.

Public system wallet: receives 100% of creator fees, buys the basket, pays holders. The dev holds no key to it in any wallet app; the key lives only on the server.

COMING SOON

COMING SOON. The draw is not live. When it is turned on it pays one SOL prize to one winner and rolls the pot over until it reaches 0.1 SOL. It also rolls over when fewer than 100 eligible holders qualify.

Daily rewards

You earn for every full hour you hold, starting after your first hour. Rewards are paid once a day at 00:10 UTC (01:10 Rome) for the day that just ended.

Rewards accrue with the holding score and are paid once a day in the basket. If your share of a coin is worth less than 0.00151384 SOL (the cost of opening a token account), it is not sent yet: it stays yours and is added to the next day, until it is worth sending. The cost of opening your token account is paid by the system wallet, from the holders' 67%.

Payouts are automatic. Once a day a bot sends holder rewards from the public system wallet. The dev does not sign, pick or change any payment. Every payout is public on-chain and listed on this page. The only manual control is an emergency stop that pauses payouts for everyone; any use is announced.

Team wallets (about 2% of supply) earn no rewards: creator E8Jg…4uXJ and dev share GscV…JEks.

You need at least 1/1,000,000 of the supply for rewards to count. The multiplier changes your share of the fees that were actually collected.

Holding multiplier

Nothing before 1h. The multiplier is 1× from 1h to 1h30, 1.5× at 1h45, 2× at 2h, 3× at 2h30, 4× at 3h and 5× at 4h, then rises with the logarithm of holding time to 10× at 30 days.
