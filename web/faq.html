FAQ

What is GTASP10?

An index coin on Solana that tracks the top 10 memecoins of the GTA 6 story, picked every hour by public rules.

How are the coins weighted?

Weights are proportional to the square root of market cap, with an 18% cap and a 5% floor, recomputed every hour.

Who receives the fees?

0% of fees go to the team. 100% go to holders: 70% is distributed as rewards in the basket coins, and 30% goes to the draw pot.

Does the team hold tokens?

The team holds about 2% of the supply. That holding is separate from fees: 0% of fees go to the team.

When are rewards paid?

You earn for every full hour you hold, starting after your first hour. Rewards are paid once a day at 00:10 UTC (01:10 Rome) for the day that just ended. Rewards accrue with the holding score and are paid once a day in the basket. A position worth less than 0.00151384 SOL (the rent of a token account) stays pending, and no token account is opened for dust.

Do team wallets get rewards?

No. Team wallets (about 2% of supply) earn no rewards: creator E8Jg…4uXJ and dev share GscV…JEks.

Can the dev change the fees or rewards?

Not on the spot. Fees and rewards follow a public rules file. Any change is visible on GitHub and only takes effect 72 hours later, so holders always see it coming. The bots run on our server; full on-chain enforcement (multisig with time lock) is planned. See Docs.

Does the dev send the payouts?

Payouts are automatic. Once a day a bot sends holder rewards from the public system wallet. The dev does not sign, pick or change any payment. Every payout is public on-chain and listed on the Rewards page. The only manual control is an emergency stop that pauses payouts for everyone; any use is announced.

How does the holding multiplier work?

Nothing before 1h. The multiplier is 1× from 1h to 1h30, 1.5× at 1h45, 2× at 2h, 3× at 2h30, 4× at 3h and 5× at 4h, then rises with the logarithm of holding time to 10× at 30 days. It changes your share of collected fees. It does not change the size of the pot.

Is the draw live?

COMING SOON. The draw is not live. When it is turned on it pays one SOL prize to one winner and rolls the pot over until it reaches 0.1 SOL. It also rolls over when fewer than 100 eligible holders qualify.

Where is the contract address?

Not published yet. Ignore any address that is not in the official announcement.
