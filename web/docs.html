Docs

The sentences below are generated from the shipped parameters. The page and the code use the same file, web/rules.js.

Universe

Solana coins whose name, ticker or description matches the public GTA vocabulary. A coin still has to pass every filter. Manual removals are for scams only.

Filters, all of them

Age at least 4 hours Liquidity at least $50,000 Pools under $10,000 ignored Top 10 holders at most 30% Fees at least 8 SOL / 24h Bundle at most 30% Authorities revoked At least 50 traders Volume greater than 0 Market cap at most 100× liquidity

Liquidity is summed across pools of at least $10,000. Smaller pools are ignored. There is no minimum on the top-10 holder share, only the 30% ceiling.

Rank and weights

Rank uses log S = 40%·ln(market cap) + 30%·ln(liquidity) + 20%·ln(24h fees) + 10%·ln(traders). Buy weights are a separate step.

Weights are proportional to the square root of market cap, with an 18% cap and a 5% floor, recomputed every hour.

Top 10. A coin enters at rank 8 or better and leaves only past rank 12.

Fees

0% of fees go to the team. 100% go to holders: 70% is distributed as rewards in the basket coins, and 30% goes to the draw pot.

The team holds about 2% of the supply.

Rewards

Rewards accrue with the holding score and are paid once a day. Anything below the minimum payout of 0.001 SOL stays pending, and no token account is opened for dust. That minimum is never set above 0.005 SOL.

Nothing before 1h. The multiplier is 1× from 1h to 1h30, 1.5× at 1h45, 2× at 2h, 3× at 2h30, 4× at 3h and 5× at 4h, then stays at 5×.

Draw

COMING SOON. The draw is not live. When it is turned on it pays SOL prizes from the pot and rolls the pot over until it reaches 0.01 SOL. It also rolls over when fewer than 100 eligible holders qualify.

Odds follow the holding score. No holder counts more than 5% of supply, and no holder can take more than 5% of the odds. A wallet needs at least 0.01% of supply to enter the draw.

What the hourly checks cover

  • A coin whose market cap is more than 100× its liquidity is blocked on every hourly run.
  • Membership does not flip on a one-hour rank wobble: a coin enters at rank 8 or better and leaves only past rank 12.
  • A holding younger than 1 hour scores 0.
  • Draw odds are capped at 5% per holder group, and a pot below the minimum is not drawn.
  • Round-trip trades are not removed from volume.
