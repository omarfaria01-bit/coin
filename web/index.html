Rules & transparency

A public formula, recalculated every hour over the previous 24 hours.

1 · Universe

Any Solana coin with a GTA reference in its name, ticker or description: GTA, GTA 6, Grand Theft Auto, Vice City, Leonida, Lucia, Jason Duval, Rockstar, Vercetti and a broad vocabulary from across the series. Manual exclusions are for scams only.

2 · Filters (all must pass)

3 · Score

log S = 0.4·ln(MC) + 0.3·ln(Liq) + 0.2·ln(Fee) + 0.1·ln(Trader)

Log scale, so one giant coin can't drown out the rest.

4 · Entry & exit

A new coin gets in by reaching the top 8. A member drops out if it falls below 12th, or instantly if it fails a filter. Emergency removal kicks in if liquidity drops 50% within an hour or authorities are re-enabled.

5 · Weights

50% market-cap share + 50% volume share, capped at 25% per coin. With fewer than 4 members, weights are equal.

6 · Verifiable

Every hourly run publishes a hash of its parameters and inputs, so anyone can recompute the index.

FAQ

What is GTASP10?

An index coin on Solana that tracks the top 10 memecoins of the GTA 6 narrative, picked every hour by public rules.

How do holder rewards work?

Hold at least 1/1,000,000 of the supply. 70% of creator fees is distributed to holders in the basket tokens, based on your holder score and holding multiplier.

How does the hourly draw work?

Every hour, 30% of fees goes into a prize pot. Odds scale with the square root of your score, linked wallets are grouped with a 5% cap, and the randomness is verifiable. If the pot is too small, it rolls over to the next hour.

Why is the top 10 empty?

The filters are strict and data for each coin is still being collected. Coins only get in once they pass every one of them.

Where's the contract address?

Not published yet. Be wary of anyone sharing one before the official announcement.

Does the team take a cut of the fees?

No. 100% of creator fees go back to the community: 70% rewards, 30% draw. No buybacks.
